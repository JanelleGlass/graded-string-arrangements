Setting out the music…

Playback is synthesized, so treat it as a guide for notes and timing rather than a model for tone. The string sounds come from the GeneralUser GS sound bank by S. Christian Collins, and the music is drawn and played with alphaTab (alphatab.net). The bass sounds an octave below where it is written, as on a real double bass.
