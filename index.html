Levels and voices

Four levels, each with a Melody, an Inner part and a Bass Line.

1
Open Strings
No fingers.
2
Easy
First position, long notes.
3
Intermediate
The tune as written.
4
Advanced
Third position.
  • MelodyThe tune.
  • InnerThe chord in between.
  • Bass LineThe bass, on any instrument.

Playing together? One on Melody, the lowest on Bass Line, everyone else on Inner.
